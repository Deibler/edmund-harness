Everyone involved in a print sees the slice and the link. Before anyone is asked to agree to a print, the requester, the approver and whoever swaps a spool or clears the plate get the sliced view, and once it starts each gets the live page link in their own chat. `skills/printing/scripts/sliced_view.ts` saves that view as one image to send: the toolpaths in the real filament colours beside the first layer.
